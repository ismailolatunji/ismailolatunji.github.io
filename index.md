### 2) UK Road Traffic Collision Severity Modelling & Risk Factor Analysis

**Problem:** Department of Transport needed to understand what factors drive collision severity to support evidence-based road safety policy and resource allocation.

**Approach:**
- Built and compared five ML models (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, SVM, MLP, Logistic Regression) on 22,000+ UK collision records
- Applied SMOTE balancing to handle class imbalance in severity outcomes
- Created interactive Power BI dashboard mapping risk factors and geographic hotspots
- Validated model assumptions and presented findings to non-technical policy stakeholders

**Results:**
- Achieved 97% classification accuracy (Random Forest) in predicting collision severity
- Dashboard identified 5 key risk factors influencing severity outcomes
- Geospatial analysis highlighted 3 high-concentration collision zones for targeted intervention
- Model outputs directly informed road safety policy recommendations

Tech Stack: Python (Scikit-Learn, pandas), SQL, Power BI, Geospatial Analysis
